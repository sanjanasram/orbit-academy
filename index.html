NASA and this website

NASA is the United States space agency, created in 1958. Its missions, open data and public education programs are the source of the ideas in this site.

1958 NASA is created. It brings the country’s civil space work under one agency.
1969 Apollo 11 lands the first people on the Moon on July 20. Moon missions in the game follow the same orbit and delta-v thinking.
1977 Voyager 1 and 2 launch. Both use RTG power, the same idea as the RTG option in the game, and Voyager 1 later enters interstellar space.
1990 The Hubble Space Telescope launches and changes how we see the universe.
2009 The Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter launches. It is the analog for the Moon mission.
2011 to 2013 Juno and MAVEN launch. They are the real analogs for the Jupiter and Mars missions in the game.
2021 Perseverance lands in Jezero crater, and its helicopter Ingenuity makes the first powered flight on another planet. The James Webb Space Telescope launches on December 25.
2022 DART hits an asteroid to test planetary defense.

What NASA contributes here

Mission facts: the Moon, Mars and Jupiter missions are modeled on LRO, MAVEN and Juno, using rounded public figures.

Open data and education: NASA publishes mission pages, images and datasets for the public. Start at science.nasa.gov and images.nasa.gov.

Space Apps Challenge: this project was built for NASA’s International Space Apps Challenge, a yearly global hackathon that uses NASA open data (spaceappschallenge.org).
