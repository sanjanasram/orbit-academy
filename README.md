# orbit-academy
Learn how space missions work, then design and fly your own.  Orbit Academy is an interactive educational website built for the NASA International Space Apps Challenge, "Space Mission Design Game".

About the Project
Orbit Academy is an interactive learning platform created to help students explore space science in a more engaging way. It brings together space-related lessons, information about real missions, quizzes, calculators and a spacecraft design simulation.
We wanted to create something where students could do more than simply read about space. They should also get an opportunity to experiment, make decisions and understand how a space mission is planned.
How It Works
1. Student Dashboard
Users can create a profile, access their learning dashboard and keep track of their progress.
2. Learn About Space
The platform provides simple learning content to help users understand planets, space science and basic mission concepts.
3. Explore Space Missions
Users can learn about missions such as NASA's Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter (LRO), MAVEN and Juno to get an idea of how spacecraft are used to explore different parts of our solar system.
4. Test Your Knowledge
An interactive quiz allows users to check what they have learned and improve their understanding of space science.
5. Design Your Own Mission
The Mission Desk is one of the main features of Orbit Academy. Users can choose a destination such as the Moon, Mars or Jupiter and build a spacecraft by selecting its launch vehicle, propulsion system, fuel, power source, communication antenna and scientific instruments.
6. Simulate the Mission
After completing the design, the platform evaluates the spacecraft based on its weight, budget, launch capacity, power, communication and delta-v requirements. It then runs a mission simulation and provides a score based on the design.

What Makes It Useful?
Orbit Academy brings learning and practical experimentation together in one place. Students can explore real space missions, understand basic spacecraft requirements and try making their own mission plans.
The simulation also helps users see that designing a spacecraft involves balancing several factors. A choice that works well in one area may create a challenge in another.
Our goal is to make space exploration more approachable for students and encourage them to become curious about science, technology and engineering.
Note: Orbit Academy is an independent educational project and is not affiliated with or officially endorsed by NASA. Mission calculations are simplified for learning and simulation purposes.
